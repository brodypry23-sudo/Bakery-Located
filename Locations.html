Welcome to the journey planning page! Here you can find information about different destinations in Hokkaido.

Sapporo

Sapporo is the capital of Japan's northernmost island, Hokkaido, and the country's fifth-largest city. Famous for the 1972 Winter Olympics, rich miso ramen, its namesake beer, and the annual Snow Festival, it blends modern grid-patterned urban living with close mountain nature.

Otaru

Otaru is a historic harbor city in Hokkaido, Japan, located about 30 to 35 minutes northwest of Sapporo by train. Famous for its beautifully preserved brick warehouses, romantic canal, glasswork shops, music boxes, and fresh seafood markets, it is a very popular day trip destination.

Niseko

Niseko is a world-famous resort area on Japan's northern island of Hokkaido, located about 100 km southwest of Sapporo. It is globally renowned for consistent, dry, and lightweight powder snow, vibrant international dining, and traditional onsen (hot springs) backdropped by Mount Yōtei.
