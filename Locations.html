Beechworth Bakery

Founded by Tom O'Toole in the historic gold-rush town of Beechworth, it has grown to eight locations across regional Victoria and southern NSW. Famous for its Ned Kelly pies, vanilla slices, Bee Stings and Bavarian cream rings.

Lune Croissanterie

A world-renowned croissant bakery that started in Melbourne, famous for its climate-controlled glass "croissant cube," precision-engineered pastries and long daily queues. Widely ranked among the best pastry shops on earth.

Baker Bleu

A beloved Melbourne sourdough bakery known for its 48-hour fermented loaves, with locations across Cremorne, South Yarra and beyond. Regularly rated among the best bread in the city.

Brunetti

An iconic Melbourne institution dating back to 1974, known for Italian pastries, cakes, gelato and coffee. Its Carlton flagship on Lygon Street is a long-time favourite for cannoli and elaborate celebration cakes.

Laurent Bakery

Brings French boulangerie and patisserie tradition to Melbourne, offering baguettes, croissants, tarts and cakes made with traditional techniques. Consistently rated among the city's top bakeries.

Country Cob Bakery

Starting in the gold-rush town of Kyneton, this multi-award-winning bakery is famous for its pies, including Thai curry beef and caramelised pork and pepper. Now also has city stores in Springvale and Boronia.

Ket Baker

Tucked away in the small town of Wallington on the Bellarine Peninsula, this artisan bakery is celebrated for buttery sourdough croissants and creative pastries like Nutella-stuffed croissants and lemon chausson.

Rolling Pin Bakery

Famous for its award-winning surf'n'turf pie (beef topped with prawns in a garlicky, creamy sauce), which took top spot at a national pie and pastie competition. Regularly draws long queues near Geelong.

Bridgewater Bakehouse

The heart of the small town of Bridgewater-on-Loddon, near Bendigo, this bakery is famous statewide for its vanilla slice — sweet icing, flaky pastry and silky custard — as well as hearty pies.

Redbeard Bakery

Hidden down a laneway in Trentham, this beloved bakery bakes sourdough in a 75-tonne, century-old woodfired Scotch oven. Also known for excellent pies, sausage rolls and sweets.

North End Bakehouse

A Shepparton bakery whose baker Matt Aylett has taken home Australia's Best Vanilla Slice and Best Hot Cross Bun titles, beating out over 140 other entrants at the Victorian Baking Show.

Parker Pies

A 27-year-old family business on Rutherglen's main street, self-described as "Australia's Greatest Pie Shop." A staple stop for travellers through the state's north-east wine country.

Flinders Sourdough

Run by local winemakers-turned-bakers who restored a historic building and its original woodfired Scotch oven. Uses only Victorian ingredients for organic sourdough loaves, plus famous "beach buns" and fruit danishes.

Cobb Lane

A Melbourne bakery with multiple locations, well regarded alongside Baker Bleu for producing some of the city's most reliably excellent sourdough loaves and donuts.

Baker D. Chirico

A stylish Melbourne bakery known as much for its sleek packaging and presentation as for its substance, with a cult-followed whole-wheat sourdough and a daily-rotating menu.

Publique Bakery

A hidden-gem bakery with genuine Parisian flair, set up inside Melbourne's lively food markets. Known for chewy baguettes, loaded cruffins, buttery croissants and filled cronuts.

Bloomwood

An Asian-influenced viennoiserie on Exhibition Street in the Melbourne CBD, combining artful presentation with picture-perfect croissants, inventive tarts and ever-changing seasonal creations.

Woodfrog Bakery

Started small in St Kilda and has since expanded to the CBD, bayside and eastern suburbs. Built a steadfast following on fermented sourdough loaves and chewy bagels.
